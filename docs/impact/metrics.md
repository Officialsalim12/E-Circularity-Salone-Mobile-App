# Impact metrics

Count impact from the operational records. Do not publish a number you cannot trace to those records.

The method, the baselines, and the public definitions are [TBD]. The categories below are the ones the definition already names. There are no formulas, emission factors, or job counts in this note.

## Environmental

Devices diverted from unmanaged disposal. Devices repaired, reused, refurbished. Materials recovered.

"Diverted" needs a definition before anyone reports it. Collected does not mean diverted if the later path is unknown.

## Economic

Where the data is real: circular services, repair and refurbishment activity, partner activity, local businesses taking part.

"Services" has no unit. Do not report revenue. The commercial model is [TBD].

## Social

Households taking part. Community taking part. Digital inclusion. Digital inclusion has no approved indicator.

## Youth

Agents taking part, agent activity, training, and income where it can actually be measured.

Training records and income are not system requirements yet. Do not infer income from points. Point value is [TBD].

## Circularity

Lifecycle completion, repair rate, reuse rate, refurbishment rate, recycling rate.

A rate needs a denominator. A repair rate could be repaired devices over assessed devices, or over collected devices. That choice is [TBD]. Until it is made, report counts and label them as counts.

A metric definition names the source records, the time window, and what is excluded. Cancelled and failed collections are not completed pathways. Do not count one device in two end outcomes unless the metric says so. Parts recovery followed by recycling of what remains may be that case. It is [TBD]. Public reports use the approved method only.
